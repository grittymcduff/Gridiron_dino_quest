🦖 Privacy

Brady's Gridiron Dino Quest · last updated November 25, 2026

Brady's Gridiron Dino Quest is a free, personal hobby game for learning football. It has no ads, and nothing about you is sold or shared with anyone.

Playing without signing in

Your game is saved in your own browser on your device. Nothing about you is sent to us.

If you join an online room, the game gives your browser a temporary anonymous ID so friends in the same room can see your character, emotes and duels. It uses the player name you typed in the game, never your real name.

Signing in with Google (cloud save)

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, name and email address. We use them only to find your saved game so you can keep playing on another device.

Your saved game (your character, level, coins, dinosaurs and progress) is stored with your account. We don't ask Google for anything else, such as your contacts, files or calendar.

Where it's kept

Cloud saves and online rooms run on Supabase, a hosting service. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

To delete your cloud save and sign-in record, email the support address shown on the Google sign-in screen. We'll remove them. Signing out in the game keeps the save on your device only.

Kids

This game is made for young football fans. Kids can play without signing in. If a child wants cloud saves, a parent should sign in with their own Google account.
